  • New Lessons Nouns and Adjectives with Rat
    October 12, 2026

    We've looked at verbs related to raten in several previous lessons, but what about the noun der Rat? First of all, it does not refer to the animal, which is die Ratte in German. Der Rat has two related meanings, as well as an archaic meaning that we'll also examine.

    banner2 PLACEHOLDER

    Der Rat means "the advice" or "the counsel." The noun der Ratschlag is a synonym.

     

    Der Rat der Virologen ist ja eindeutig.

    The advice of virologists is clear.

    Caption 31, Coronavirus: Fernsehansprache von Angela Merkel - Part 3

     Play Caption

     

    Auch für Bitten und Ratschläge wird diese Satzform benutzt.

    This sentence form is also used for requests and advice.

    Caption 5, Deutsch mit Eylin: Der Imperativ

     Play Caption

     

    But der Rat also refers to the group of people who give advice, so it means not only "the counsel," but also "the council." There are many different types of councils in Germany, so you'll often encounter the word in compound nouns.

     

    Wir regen an, den parlamentarischen Beirat für nachhaltige Entwicklung beim Bundestag mit der Kontrolle der Nachhaltigkeitsprüfung auch zu beauftragen.

    We encourage the parliamentary council for sustainable development in the Bundestag [German Parliament] to assign the control of sustainability testing, as well.

    Captions 37-39, Angela Merkel: Beim Nachhaltigkeitsrat - Part 2

     Play Caption

     

    Welche Organisation in einer Firma hilft den Arbeitnehmern und Arbeitnehmerinnen bei Problemen mit dem Arbeitgeber oder der Arbeitgeberin? Der Betriebsrat.

    Which organization in a company helps the employees in case of problems with their employer? The works council.

    Captions 27-29, Bundesrepublik: Deutschland Einbürgerungstest

     Play Caption

     

    There are also a few related adjectives to learn: 

     

    Er war ratlos. Plötzlich hörte er Musik. Jemand spielte auf einem Dudelsack.

    He was at a loss. Suddenly, he heard music. Someone was playing the bagpipes.

    Caption 20, Märchen - Sagenhaft: Hans mein Igel

     Play Caption

     

    Bei nassen Fahrbahnen, etwa nach Regen ist es beispielsweise ratsam, abrupter aufzukommen.

    On wet runways, for example after rain, it is advisable to land more abruptly.

    Captions 18-20, Flugreisen: Was dir Fluggesellschaften nicht verraten…

     Play Caption

     

    Finally, you may have seen the nouns der Hausrat and der Vorrat. These relate to an archaic meaning of the word der Rat, which is a store of provisions, means, or help. 

     

    Schön und gut, nur jedes Mal den halben Hausrat zum Camping mitzuschleppen ...

    Fine and dandy, but dragging around half of the household supplies every time...

    Caption 23, Glamping: Camping mit Stil

     Play Caption

     

    Der fertige Honig dient der aktuellen Ernährung des Bienenvolkes, der Aufzucht der Brut und als Vorrat für den Winter.

    The finished honey is used as day-to-day nutrition for the bee colony, for raising the brood, and as a supply for the winter.

    Captions 31-32, Für Tierfreunde Aktion Tier: Die Honigbiene

     Play Caption

    Have a look at our previous lessons on erraten vs. raten and other related verbs. And as always, you'll find additional examples on Yabla German.

  • Lessons Schief and Related Verbs
    October 5, 2026

    As an adjective or adverb, the word schief essentially means "crooked" or "askew." In these first two examples, it is used to describe a painting hanging on a wall: 
     

    Das hängt doch nicht schief!

    That's not crooked!

    Caption 25, Die Pfefferkörner: Alles auf Anfang

     Play Caption

     

    Noch schiefer. So was von schief.

    Even more crooked. So crooked.

    Caption 27, Die Pfefferkörner: Alles auf Anfang

     Play Caption

    banner4 PLACEHOLDER

    But schief can refer to more than just physical position. For example, here it describes the way someone looks at another person, suggesting a strange, suspicious, or disapproving look:

     

    Er tauchte direkt unter dir ab. -Er kuckte mich auch ganz schief an. -Ja.

    He dove down directly under you. -He looked very askance at me, too. -Yes.

    Caption 23, Abenteuer Nordsee: Unter Riesenhaien und Tintenfischen - Part 2

     Play Caption

     

    The common verbs schieflaufen and schiefgehen essentially have the same meaning:

     

    Jeder normale oder vernünftige Zauberer würde jetzt abbrechen, denn es können eine Menge Dinge schieflaufen.

    Any normal and reasonable magician would now call it off, because, a lot of things can go wrong.

    Captions 55-56, Magie: Die Zaubershow

     Play Caption

     

    OK, da ist wohl was schiefgelaufen.

    OK, something went wrong there indeed.

    Caption 30, Nachhaltige Ernährung: Animation

     Play Caption

     

    Ich wusste ja ganz genau, dass nichts schiefgehen würde.

    I knew perfectly well that nothing would go wrong.

    Captions 19-20, Jan von München: Das tanzende Pferd

     Play Caption

     

    Es sind viele Dinge schiefgegangen.

    A lot of things went wrong.

    Caption 42, Sabine und Ivana Sabines Weltreise

     Play Caption

  • Lessons Contrastive and Corrective Connectors, Part II
    September 28, 2026

    Last week, we looked at how words like sondern, vielmehr, and stattdessen are used not only to correct statements, but also to refine them. This week, we'll continue with some words that are used to compare and contrast: hingegen, dagegen, and im Gegenteil. 

    banner4 PLACEHOLDER

    Depending on the context, you might see hingegen translated as "by contrast," "in comparison," or "on the other hand."

     

    In Deutschland hingegen sind es nur zwei pro tausend Einwohner. 

    In Germany, by contrast, there are just two per thousand residents. 

    Caption 52, Flüchtlingskrise: 10 Vorurteile, die nicht stimmen

     Play Caption

     

    Radfahrer müssen sich hingegen vor allem ihrer Verletzlichkeit bewusst werden.

    Cyclists must, on the other hand, above all, become more conscious of their vulnerability.

    Caption 32, Crashtest: Fahrradfahrer profitieren kaum vom Fußgängerschutz am Auto

     Play Caption

     

    The word dagegen can be used to express being against something, and the first example below shows how it expresses opposition to something that was previously mentioned. The second example shows its contrastive function, which is very similar to how hingegen is used. 

     

    Und andere wieder benutzen dagegen ein Nasenspray.

    And others use a nasal spray against it.

    Caption 21, Deutsch mit Eylin: Erkältungszeit

     Play Caption

     

    Im See drin ist es dagegen verhältnismäßig warm, denn das Eis isoliert.

    Inside the lake, it is warm in comparison, because the ice insulates.

    Caption 13, Alpenseen: Kühle Schönheiten

     Play Caption

     

    The word das Gegenteil means "the opposite," so im Gegenteil essentially means "on the contrary." It can be used in a sentence, but also on its own.

     

    Er ist im Gegenteil sogar ein sehr nützlicher Waldbewohner.

    On the contrary, it is actually a very valuable forest dweller.

    Caption 33, Die letzten Paradiese: Die Schönheit der Alpen

     Play Caption

     

    Im Gegenteil. In vielen trendigen Cafés ist Filterkaffee wieder richtig angesagt.

    On the contrary. In many trendy cafés, filter coffee is really popular again.

    Caption 14, Eva zeigt uns: Wie man Kaffee kocht

     Play Caption

     

    Further Learning

    Look out for hingegen, dagegen, and im Gegenteil in Yabla German videos, and pay attention to how they are used in context.

  • Lessons Contrastive and Corrective Connectors, Part I
    September 21, 2026

    This week, we'll look at words that have a negating and/or corrective function. These may simply be used to communicate the idea "X is wrong, but Y is correct," or they may be used to clarify or refine a statement. Let's look at the German words sondern, vielmehr, eher, and stattdessen.

    The conjunction sondern connects two contrasting elements and generally comes directly after the comma separating them. Together with nicht or keine, sondern serves to negate and correct the first statement made in the sentence. 

     

    Es sind aber keine Pflanzen, sondern winzige Tierchen, Einzeller.

    These are not plants, but rather tiny little animals, single-celled organisms.

    Caption 46, Alpenseen Kühle: Schönheiten - Part 6

     Play Caption

     

    Ihr eigentliches Heimatland ist nicht Deutschland, sondern Österreich.

    Her real homeland is not Germany, but Austria.

    Caption 3, Christina Stürmer: Wir leben den Moment

     Play Caption

     

    The word vielmehr doesn't necessarily require negation and can also be translated as "in fact" or "more precisely." 

     

    Ich glaube vielmehr, dass es große Insekten sind.

    I rather think they are big insects.

    Caption 16, Es war einmal... der Weltraum: Die Saurier

     Play Caption

     

    Mittlerweile weiß man, dass sie vielmehr Relikte aus der Entstehungszeit unseres Sonnensystems sind.

    In the meantime we know that they are more likely relics from the time of origin of our solar system.

    Captions 20-21, Unser Universum: Asteroiden - Gefahr aus dem All?

     Play Caption

     

    In addition to meaning "earlier"/"sooner" and "more"/"to a greater extent," eher can also have a corrective or contrastive function and can even be combined with sondern.

     

    Straßenlage ist aber auch im Land der unbegrenzten Möglichkeiten nicht die Stärke des Trabi, da geht es eher gemütlich zu.

    [Its] road handling ability, however, even in the land of unlimited possibilities, is not a strength of the Trabi, there instead it runs along quite leisurely.

    Captions 39-40, Der Trabi: Das Kultauto aus dem Osten

     Play Caption

     

    Diese Fische scheinen uns gegenüber nicht scheu, sondern eher neugierig zu sein.

    These fish do not seem to be shy towards us, but rather curious.

    Caption 67, Evolution: Meeresbewohner

     Play Caption

     

    Another relevant word is stattdessen ("instead"), which can also be combined with sondern:

     

    Entgegen der geläufigen Meinung robben die kalifornischen Seelöwen nicht, wenn sie sich fortbewegen. Stattdessen können sie ihre Hinterflossen geschickt unter ihren Körper drehen.

    Contrary to common belief, California sea lions don't crawl when they move around. Instead, they can skillfully turn their hind flippers underneath their body.

    Captions 24-27, Evolution: Die Küsten

     Play Caption

     

    Für die Reduktion von Plastik benutze ich im Alltag keine Plastiktüten bei meinem Einkauf, sondern nehme stattdessen einen Stoffbeutel oder einen eigenen Rucksack mit.

    For the reduction of plastic, I don't use plastic bags for my everyday shopping, but instead take a cloth bag or my own backpack with me.

    Captions 19-21, Deutsch mit Paula: Nachhaltigkeit

     Play Caption

     

    Further Learning

    You will find these words in many videos on Yabla German.

  • Lessons Political Action
    September 14, 2026

    With elections occurring this month in several German federal states, political action is currently a relevant topic. What are the options when a population is unhappy with the decisions of the current leadership? Protests, rallies, and marches are very common in Germany. The Berlin city website has a list of demonstrations taking place on any given day. 

    banner3 PLACEHOLDER

    The word for "protest" in German is die Demonstration, and it's often shortened to die Demo. 

     

    Die Wege der beiden Männer kreuzten sich bei der entscheidenden Demonstration.

    The paths of the two men crossed at the crucial protest.

    Caption 39, 30 Jahre Mauerfall Deutschland: 30 Jahre nach dem Fall der Berliner Mauer - Part 1

     Play Caption

     

    Zu Beginn der Demo hatten wir uns noch untergehakt.

    At the beginning of the demonstration we still had our arms linked.

    Caption 3, 30 Jahre Mauerfall Deutschland: 30 Jahre nach dem Fall der Berliner Mauer - Part 2

     Play Caption

     

    Protests often involve a rally:

     

    Bei der Kundgebung in Sichtweite der Baustelle [Politik für heute statt von gestern]

    At the rally within sight of the construction site [Politics for today instead of from yesterday]

    Caption 6, Klimaschutz Protest gegen Ausbau von Stadtautobahn A100

     Play Caption

     

    The German word der Umzug can mean "parade" or "procession." A protest march can be referred to more specifically as der Demonstrationszug.

     

    Und auch jetzt Kinderfasching oder am Dienstag in Eggenstein den Umzug?

    And also now at Children's Carnival or on Tuesday at the parade in Eggenstein?

    Caption 43, Fasching: mit Cettina

     Play Caption

     

    Mit einem Demonstrationszug entlang des Terminal eins machten rund zweitausend Gewerkschaftler lautstark auf ihre Forderungen aufmerksam.

    With a protest march along Terminal One, around two thousand trade unionists loudly called attention to their demands.

    Captions 27-28, Rheinmain im Blick Erneut Streik am Frankfurter Flughafen

     Play Caption

     

    For people who can't travel to the protest, there is often a petition that can be signed:

     

    Wir würden uns freuen, wenn alle Menschen auf PETA de [www.peta.de] unsere Onlinepetition unterstützen,

    We would be happy, if all people would support our online petition on PETA de [www.peta.de],

    Caption 13, PETA-Aktion: Gegen das Wal-Massaker

     Play Caption

  • Lessons Describing Comfort in German
    September 7, 2026

    The idea of comfort can be expressed in many different ways in German. One of the most common adjectives for the word "comfortable" is bequem, which can refer to physical comfort or convenience. It can describe clothing and furniture, but also ways of doing things and situations that make life easier.

    banner2 PLACEHOLDER

    Am besten mit dem Zug. Das ist schnell und ist bequem.

    The best way is by train. It's fast and comfortable.

    Captions 13-14, Sabine und Ivana: planen eine Reise nach Nürnberg

     Play Caption

     

    Sieht bequem aus. -Na los, probier die Sachen an.

    Looks comfortable. -Well go, try these things on.

    Caption 14, Nicos Weg A1 Folge 60: Schick!

     Play Caption

     

    Gemütlich suggests coziness and a warm, inviting atmosphere rather than simply physical comfort. You might describe an apartment or a relaxed evening as gemütlich.

     

    Mach es dir gemütlich und nimm dir Zeit.

    Make yourself comfortable and take your time.

    Caption 6, Deutsch für Anfänger #2: Begrüßen und verabschieden

     Play Caption

     

    Die Küche ist sehr gemütlich, hier sitzen wir oft.

    The kitchen is very comfortable, we sit here often.

    Caption 30, Nicos Weg A1 Folge 26: Meine Wohnung

     Play Caption

     

    Behaglich is close to gemütlich but slightly more intimate, suggesting contentment, while wohnlich describes a room or other space that feels inviting as a place to live. Komfortabel suggests a high level of comfort, often thanks to good facilities or equipment, and can sometimes imply a degree of luxury.

     

    Und gerade diese Kombination von sportlich und komfortabel gefällt mir besonders gut am F-Pace.

    And precisely this combination of sporty and comfortable is what I like especially well about the F-Pace.

    Caption 10, Auto-Bild-TV: Tops & Flops der IAA

     Play Caption

     

    Finally, there is the adjective angenehm, which is broader and means pleasant or agreeable.

     

    Ein Schlag mit der gewaltigen Schwanzflosse wäre sicher nicht angenehm.

    A blow from the powerful tail fin certainly wouldn't be pleasant.

    Caption 28, Abenteuer Nordsee: Unter Riesenhaien und Tintenfischen

     Play Caption

    You'll find these words used in various contexts on Yabla German!

  • Lessons Vertrauen and sich trauen
    August 30, 2026

    In German, the main noun for "trust" is das Vertrauen.

    banner6 PLACEHOLDER

    Beim Canyoning spielen Teamgeist und das gegenseitige Vertrauen eine wichtige Rolle.

    When canyoning, team spirit and mutual trust play an important role.

    Caption 22, Die letzten Paradiese: Die Schönheit der Alpen 2

     Play Caption

     

    The verb vertrauen ("to trust") takes the dative case. To say "to trust one another," you can use sich vertrauen, with the reflexive pronoun sich having a reciprocal meaning.

     

    Also, man sollte sich gegenseitig auf jeden Fall vertrauen.

    So, you should definitely trust each other.

    Caption 11, Die Alpen von oben: S1 E2 Von Graz ins Ausseerland

     Play Caption

     

    Bist du dir sicher, dass du ihr vertrauen kannst?

    Are you sure you can trust her?

    Caption 41, Die Pfefferkörner: Das Wunderkind

     Play Caption

     

    As you might remember from our lesson on marriage, both trauen and sich trauen can be used when talking about getting married. But in other contexts, sich trauen means "to dare" or "to have the courage to do something."

     

    Aber ob sie sich das traut?

    But will she dare to do it?

    Caption 20, Max Giesinger: Wenn sie tanzt

     Play Caption

     

    The verb anvertrauen means to entrust something to another person’s care. Then, there is also sich jemandem anvertrauen, meaning to confide in someone. 

     

    Jetzt können wir ihnen eine richtige Aufgabe anvertrauen.

    Now we can entrust them with a real task.

    Caption 36, Es war einmal... der Weltraum: Planet Omega - Part 1

     Play Caption

     

    Finally, the adjective vertraut is used to refer to someone or something trusted or familiar. 

     

    Die Landschaft ist ihm von Geburt an vertraut.

    The landscape has been familiar to him since birth.

    Caption 19, Die Alpen von oben: S01 E03 Von Salzburg zum Königssee

     Play Caption

     

    Further Learning

    You'll find many more examples of vertrauen, trauen, and related words on Yabla German!

  • Lessons Bread
    August 23, 2026

    This week's lesson is devoted to an integral part of German culture: bread!

    banner2 PLACEHOLDER

    In German bakeries, you will find both loaves and rolls for sale. The German word for a loaf is der Laib, but in everyday language Germans commonly just say ein Brot for "a loaf of bread," as you can see in this example:

     

    Frieda hat ein Brot gekauft.

    Frieda bought a loaf of bread.

    Caption 20, Deutsch mit Eylin: Das Perfekt

     Play Caption

     

    There are, however, many different words for "bread roll" depending on what region of Germany you are in. In particular, die Schrippe is used in Berlin and the surrounding region, and die Semmel in Bavaria and Austria. 

     

    Ich würde auch manchmal morgens Brötchen holen.

    I would also go out to get bread rolls in the morning

    Caption 32, AnnenMayKantereit: 3. Stock

     Play Caption

     

    „Schrippe“. Das ist das Berliner Wort für „Brötchen“.

    "Bread roll." That's the Berlin word for "bread roll."

    Captions 16-17, Deutsch mit Giada: In einer Bäckerei

     Play Caption

     

    Das Zeug geht weg wie warme Semmeln.

    The stuff is selling like warm rolls [idiom, selling quickly].

    Caption 11, Die Pfefferkörner: Alles auf Anfang

     Play Caption

     

    The many types of German bread can be divided into several main categories according to the flour used. Bread generally contains wheat flour (das Weizenmehl), but not exclusively, since rye flour (das Roggenmehl) is very popular. 

     

    Ja, ich denke, ich nehme ein halbes Weißbrot und ein halbes Vollkornbrot.

    Yes, I think I'll take half a loaf of white bread and half a loaf of whole wheat bread.

    Caption 9, Nicos Weg: Was darf es sein?

     Play Caption

     

    Und dazu eine Scheibe frisches Roggenbrot mit kalter Butter.

    And with it, a slice of fresh rye bread with cold butter.

    Caption 8, Deutsch mit Eylin: Geschmack beschreiben

     Play Caption

     

    In the last example, you can see the word for a slice of bread. When ordering at a bakery, it's also useful to know how to ask for half a loaf:

     

    Die Hälfte. Ein halbes Roggenbrot kostet einen Euro sechzig.

    Half. Half a loaf of rye bread costs one euro sixty.

    Caption 6, Nicos Weg A1 Folge 50: Was darf es sein?

     Play Caption

     

    Further Learning

    For more vocabulary and phrases, watch Giada's video about visiting a German bakery on Yabla German!

  • Lessons Talking About Success and Failure
    August 17, 2026

    The basic German word for "success" is der Erfolg. The adjective form is erfolgreich. 

    banner2 PLACEHOLDER

     

    Ich wünsche euch viel Erfolg bei der Prüfung.

    I wish you much success at the exam.

    Caption 42, Bundesrepublik: Deutschland Einbürgerungstest

     Play Caption

     

    Aber hier in Deutschland ist sie mindestens genauso bekannt und erfolgreich.

    But here in Germany she is at least as famous and successful.

    Caption 4, Christina Stürmer: Wir leben den Moment

     Play Caption

     

    Um Erfolg zu haben, müssen sie schneller als die Fische sein.

    To be successful, they have to be faster than the fish.

    Caption 16, Expeditionen ins Tierreich Die Feldberger Seen

     Play Caption

     

    The verb gelingen is also used in connection with success. Take a careful look at how the sentence is structured:

     

    Ich hoffe, euch sind die Quarkkeulchen auch gelungen.

    I hope you also succeeded in making the quark dumplings.

    Caption 46, Bundesländer und ihre Rezepte: Sachsen

     Play Caption

     

    "To fail" as in "to fail a test" is expressed with the verb durchfallen. You can also say nicht bestehen ("to not pass").

     

    Sie ist in fast allen Prüfungen des Trainerkurses durchgefallen.

    She failed almost all of the exams in the trainer course.

    Caption 73, Yabla Gerichtshof: Das verzogene Hündchen

     Play Caption

     

    The verb scheitern indicates that a goal, plan, or attempt is not achieved.

     

    Allerdings scheiterte sein Fluchtversuch. Die Konsequenz: das DDR-Gefängnis.

    However, his attempt to flee failed. The consequence: GDR [German Democratic Republic] prison.

    Caption 7, 25 Jahre Mauerfall: Radtour durch die Geschichte

     Play Caption

     

    The verb versagen is more negative and indicates a failure to function or perform properly. It can be used for people, institutions, systems, or even parts of the body.

     

    Er läuft Runden im Park, bis die Beine versagen.

    He runs laps in the park until his legs fail.

    Caption 21, Maxim Meine Soldaten

     Play Caption

     

    Further Learning

  • Lessons "To Miss" and "To Miss Out"
    August 9, 2026

    In German, there are several ways to express the meanings of both verbs above.

    banner4 PLACEHOLDER

    When we speak about wishing for something that is absent, there are two verbs that are often used. First, there is vermissen. But it's also possible to use a construction with a dative object and the verb fehlen, which literally means "to be lacking" or "to be missing."

     

    Ich habe dich mein halbes Leben vermisst.

    I've missed you half my life.

    Caption 33, Clueso: Mond feat. Elif

     Play Caption

     

    Ihr habt mir auch gefehlt.

    I've missed you too.

    Caption 37, Heidi: Dunkle Vergangenheit

     Play Caption

     

    Das deutsche Brot fehlt mir, ich vermisse das deutsche Brot.

    I miss the German bread, I miss the German bread.

    Caption 28, Deutschkurs in Blaubeuren: Relativsätze mit Präpositionen

     Play Caption


     

    The verb verpassen means "to miss" or "to miss out" in the sense of failing to catch, attend, or take advantage of something. It's not about feeling someone's or something's absence.

     

    Es macht also nichts, wenn man eine Bahn verpasst hat.

    So, it doesn't matter if you have missed a train.

    Caption 35, Deutsch mit Eylin: Öffentliche Verkehrsmittel in Hamburg

     Play Caption

     

    The verb versäumen is similar to verpassen, but can have a stronger connotation of having neglected or failed to do something.

     

    Sie haben schon wieder Ihren Gesprächstermin versäumt.

    You've missed your appointment yet again.

    Caption 7, Die Pfefferkörner: Alles auf Anfang

     Play Caption

     

    Ich möchte keinen Tag versäumen.

    I don't want to miss a day.

    Caption 29, Emilio: Find Ich Dich

     Play Caption

     

    A more complex construction is sich entgehen lassen. The verb entgehen means "to escape" or "to evade," so it's "to let something escape you." 

     

    Auch Natali will sich das nicht entgehen lassen.

    Natali doesn't want to let herself miss out on this either.
